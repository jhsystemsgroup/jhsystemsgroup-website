How it works

Three steps, then it runs on its own.

  1. They sign up at checkout

    A short form at your counter or online. Customers add who they buy for and the dates that matter: birthdays, anniversaries, Mother's Day.

  2. A reminder goes out

    Two days before each date, they get a friendly text or email from your shop asking if they'd like flowers for pickup or delivery.

  3. You get the order

    Your shop receives an email with who it's for, the occasion, the date, pickup or delivery, and notes like favorite colors or flowers to avoid.

Questions

What florists ask first.

Do my customers stay mine?

Yes. The system runs under your shop's name, the reminders come from your shop, and orders come straight to you. JH Systems Group is not a wire service or marketplace.

Do I need to learn new software?

No. I set everything up and test it with you. Your day-to-day is reading order emails and making beautiful arrangements.

How long does setup take?

Most of the work happens on my end. We'll set a timeline on our first call so you're collecting dates as soon as possible, ideally well before Valentine's Day.

How do customers pay?

We'll set this up around how your shop already works, whether that's a secure payment link or charging the card you have on file.
